03 · THE UNIT-ECONOMICS CHECK

Ten times the tests.
Half the reagent?

Automation changes the denominator. Smaller volumes and denser plates can reduce materials per test, even when experimental activity expands. Move the inputs and see the arithmetic.

Baseline: 100k tests · 100 µL · 96 wells · 1 tip/test.
Full plates; dead volume, controls, retests, ASP and services excluded.

MATERIAL QUANTITIES · BASELINE = 1×

10× more experiments, but only 0.5× the reagent volume.

Net purchasing∝Experimental activity×Materials per test×Net price & mix
